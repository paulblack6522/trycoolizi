Run the Google Ads campaigns for your own online store, without the clutter.

Coolizi Ads Desk is the in-house web tool we use to manage the Google Ads campaigns for our store, and we offer it to other small shops that run their own Google Ads. You connect your account with Google sign-in and work on campaigns, ad groups, keywords and ads from one simple screen.

How it works

  1. Sign in with your Google account and allow Coolizi Ads Desk to manage your Google Ads campaigns.
  2. Pick the Google Ads account you want to work on. Only accounts your Google login can already access are shown.
  3. Review reports, prepare changes, and apply them when you confirm.

One table for everything

Campaigns, ad groups, keywords, ads and search terms with clicks, impressions, cost and conversions.

New campaigns in minutes

Set budget, bidding, locations, languages, ad groups, keywords and ads in one form.

Safe changes

Changes are checked by Google first and applied only after you confirm.

Pause and tidy up

Pause, enable, edit or remove campaigns, ads and keywords, and add negative keywords.

Your data

Coolizi Ads Desk uses the Google Ads API only to show you your own accounts and to make the changes you ask for. It does not sell your data, does not use it for advertising and does not share it with anyone. You can disconnect at any time from your Google Account settings, and we delete your stored access on request. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Coolizi Ads Desk's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
