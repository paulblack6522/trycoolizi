Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains how Coolizi Ads Desk (trycoolizi.com/ads-desk) handles information when you use the service.

What we access

  • Google Ads data. When you sign in with Google and grant the Google Ads permission (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords), Coolizi Ads Desk can read the Google Ads accounts your login can access, their campaigns, ad groups, keywords, ads, budgets, targeting and performance reports, and can make changes you request.
  • Access token. Google gives us a token that lets the service act for you. We do not receive or store your Google password.
  • We do not request your email address, contacts, files or any other Google data.

How we use it

  • Only to show you your own Google Ads information and to carry out the changes you confirm.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising, profiling, or training AI models.
  • No person reads your account data except when you ask us for support, or when the law requires it.

Coolizi Ads Desk's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted on our server and are never shown in the app or written to logs. Report data is fetched from Google when you view it and is kept only as long as needed to display it.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your access token until you disconnect. You can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have all stored data deleted, email support@trycoolizi.com; we delete it within 30 days.

Cookies

These pages use no tracking or advertising cookies. The app uses one session cookie to keep you signed in.

Children

Coolizi Ads Desk is for business users and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above and post the new version on this page.
